### Common Items

**Noxian Steel Blade** Reliable Noxian military issue. Well-balanced and durable.

- _Effect:_ +1 weapon, non magical

---

### Uncommon Items

**Crimson Band** Red cloth armband worn by Crimson Circle initiates.

- _Effect:_ Can damage yourself to gain +1d6 to next damage roll

**Shuriman Scarab Charm** Small carved beetle on leather cord, ancient Shuriman protective talisman.

- _Effect:_ +1 to saving throws

**Petricite Weapon** Any made out of petricite.

- _Effect:_ On hit, target has disadvantage on next spell attack roll or concentration check

**Elixir of Iron** Noxian alchemical concoction, grants temporary toughness.

- _Effect:_ Temporary HP and advantage on Constitution saves for 1 hour

**Doublestrike Gauntlets** Fighting gloves reinforced with metal plates.

- _Effect:_ Once per short rest, make an extra melee attack as part of Attack action

**Ruby Crystal Amulet** Red gemstone that enhances vitality.

- _Effect:_ Increases max HP by small amount

**Boots of Swiftness** Light leather boots that enhance mobility.

- _Effect:_ +10 ft movement speed, ignore difficult terrain from non-magical sources

**Null-Magic Mantle** Simple gray cloak with subtle anti-magic weave.

- _Effect:_ Resistance to one damage type from spells (determined when found)

**Sapphire Crystal Ring** Blue gemstone ring that aids concentration.

- _Effect:_ Advantage on concentration checks for spells

**Cloth Armor Padding** Extra padding that can be added to existing armor.

- _Effect:_ +1 AC

**Long Sword** Exceptionally well-crafted longsword, Demacian or Noxian make.

- _Effect:_ +1 magic weapon

---

### Rare Items

**Heartsteel Fragment** Piece of legendary armor that grows with its bearer.

- _Effect:_ Gain +1 max HP permanently for each significant enemy defeated (cap at +10)

**Spirit Visage Mask** White ceramic half-mask that enhances healing.

- _Effect:_ All healing received increased by 25%, +2 to Wisdom saving throws

**Sunfire Aegis** Shield that burns with internal heat.

- _Effect:_ +1 shield, enemies within 5ft take minor fire damage at start of your turn

**Demacian Steel Longsword** Famed rune-steel blade, holds edge perfectly.

- _Effect:_ +2 weapon, never dulls or breaks, advantage on attempts to break enemy weapons

**Randuin's Omen** Heavy shield with time-manipulation enchantment.

- _Effect:_ +1 shield, when hit in melee you can use reaction to slow attacker (half movement next turn)

**Bramble Vest** Armor with magical thorns that punish attackers.

- _Effect:_ +1 armor, reflects portion of melee damage back to attacker (like 1d6)

**Hextech Protobelt** Piltover magitech device that propels wearer forward.

- _Effect:_ Once per short rest, dash 20ft in any direction as a bonus action, deal damage in a cone

**Nashor's Tooth Dagger** Curved dagger with enhanced magical properties.

- _Effect:_ Spellcasters only - spells deal additional 1d6 force damage

**Lich Bane Sword** Blade that pulses with arcane energy after spellcasting.

- _Effect:_ After casting spell, next melee attack deals bonus damage equal to spell level

---

### Very Rare Items

**Infinity Edge** Perfectly balanced blade that strikes true.

- _Effect:_ +2 weapon, critical hits deal maximum damage + roll additional damage dice

**Black Cleaver Axe** Heavy axe that rends through armor with repeated strikes.

- _Effect:_ Consecutive hits on same target reduce their AC by 1 (max -3, lasts until end of combat)

**Warmog's Armor** Regenerating plate armor of legendary make.

- _Effect:_ +1 armor, regenerate HP when out of combat if below half HP

**Morellonomicon** Book that inflicts grievous wounds.

- _Effect:_ +2 staff/arcane focus, spell attacks reduce target's healing received by half for 1 minute

**Guardian Angel Talisman** Holy talisman that protects from death.

- _Effect:_ Once per week, if reduced to 0 HP, instead return to half HP (auto-activates)

**Zhonya's Hourglass** Golden hourglass that stops time for bearer.

- _Effect:_ Once per long rest, become invulnerable and unable to act for 1 round

**Force of Nature Cloak** Cloak woven from elemental essence.

- _Effect:_ Resistance to all elemental damage types (fire, cold, lightning, acid)

### Legendary Items

**Trinity Force Weapon** (very endgame Act 1) Weapon that channels three forms of power.

- _Effect:_ +3 weapon, after using ability/spell, next attack deals bonus damage and applies slow effect

**Rabadon's Deathcap** Impossibly tall pointed hat of legendary arcanist.

- _Effect:_ Increase spell save DC by 2, all spell damage increased by 25%
