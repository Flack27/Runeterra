### Void Creatures

**Voidlings** (Swarm, CR 1/4) _Environment: Stonewall village, near Void rift_ Small quadrupedal creatures, size of medium dogs. Pale violet-white flesh with insectoid chitinous legs. No visible eyes but track movement perfectly. Hunt in packs of 4-8. Make disturbing chittering sounds.

- _Behavior:_ Aggressive, attracted to warmth and movement
- _Combat:_ Low individual HP, fast movement, pack tactics
- _Loot:_ Void-touched chitin pieces (crafting material)

**Void Hauler** (Hovering Carrier, CR 2) _Environment: Stonewall village, between bodies and shrine_ Grotesque floating jellyfish-like creature with translucent purple membrane and 6-8 writhing tentacles. Size of large barrel. Pulsates with sickly bioluminescence. Emits low humming sound.

- _Behavior:_ Carries humanoid corpses to shrine to feed Broodmother, avoids direct combat if possible
- _Combat:_ Hovering (15ft off ground), tentacle grab attacks, can carry one medium creature
- _Special:_ If threatened while carrying body, drops it to flee or fight
- _Loot:_ Void membrane (alchemical ingredient)

**Immature Void Broodmother** (Boss, CR 4-5) _Environment: Ancient Shuriman shrine beneath Stonewall_ Massive creature size of a wagon, still developing. Pale flesh hardening into dark purple-black chitin plates. Multiple eyes opening across body. Surrounded by half-devoured corpses.

- _Behavior:_ Currently feeding to mature, territorial but not at full strength
- _Combat:_ High HP, spawns 1-2 Voidlings each round, corrosive bite, vulnerable to fire
- _Phase Change:_ At half HP becomes more aggressive and faster
- _Loot:_ Broodmother chitin plate (rare armor material), concentrated Void essence

**Void-Touched Bat Swarm** (Environmental, CR 1/2) _Environment: Cave systems, dark corners of Stonewall_ Normal cave bats corrupted by Void proximity. Eyes glow faint purple, move with unsettling coordination.

- _Behavior:_ More aggressive than normal bats, attack in coordinated swarm
- _Combat:_ Swarm mechanics, minor disease/corruption risk on hits
- _Loot:_ None (corrupted flesh decays immediately)

### Noxian Enemies

**Noxian Deserters** (Variable, CR 1-2) _Environment: Roads, wilderness, hiding near settlements_ Soldiers who fled Ionian war. Mixture of combat veterans and traumatized wrecks. Noxian military gear in poor condition, mix of desperation and skill.

- _Behavior:_ Some just want to survive, others turned to banditry. May negotiate.
- _Combat:_ Professional military training, varied tactics depending on mental state
- _Loot:_ Noxian military equipment, personal effects, sometimes maps or letters

**Legion Marauder** (Aggressive, CR 2) _Environment: Roads, borderlands, wherever Trifarian Legion campaigns_ Brutal soldiers who follow the Legion to loot and prey on the weak. Scavengers in Noxian colors but not official military.

- _Behavior:_ Opportunistic bullies, attack weak targets, flee from superior force
- _Combat:_ Hit hard but fragile, become stronger when multiple are present (pack tactics)
- _Loot:_ Stolen goods, Noxian weapons

**Basilisk Rider** (Mounted, CR 3) _Environment: Noxian military roads, important crossroads_ Soldier mounted on war lizard (smaller cousin of siege basilisks). Both rider and mount dangerous.

- _Behavior:_ Patrol roads, enforce Noxian authority, pursue deserters
- _Combat:_ Mounted combat advantages, basilisk has powerful bite, rider uses lance/bow
- _Mount:_ If rider dies, basilisk may flee or go berserk depending on training
- _Loot:_ Quality Noxian military gear, basilisk scales (armor material)

**Trifarian Assessor** (Elite Scout, CR 3) _Environment: Anywhere Noxus has interest, often alone or with small squad_ Elite scout/recruiter who identifies threats and talent for the Legion. Wears light armor, highly mobile.

- _Behavior:_ Evaluates threats before engaging, reports to superiors, may try to recruit worthy opponents
- _Combat:_ Hit-and-run tactics, mobility focused, calls for backup if outmatched
- _Special:_ Carries signal flare to summon reinforcements
- _Loot:_ Quality light armor, Trifarian identification papers, signals

### Crimson Circle (Blood Cultists)

**Crimson Disciple** (Cultist, CR 1) _Environment: Hidden shrines, city underbelly, secret meeting places_ Initiate of Vladimir's blood magic cult. Pale skin, wears red robes, practices self-harm rituals.

- _Behavior:_ Fanatical devotion, seeks to spread blood magic teachings
- _Combat:_ Damages self to power attacks, surprisingly durable despite wounds
- _Special:_ Becomes more dangerous as fight progresses (loses HP, gains power)
- _Loot:_ Blood ritual implements, crimson robes, disturbing texts

**Crimson Curator** (Blood Mage, CR 3) _Environment: Underground temples, forgotten crypts, anywhere blood magic is practiced_ Experienced blood mage who collects "specimens." Gaunt, pale, carries vials of blood.

- _Behavior:_ Calculative, studies opponents, wants to capture not kill (for experiments)
- _Combat:_ Blood magic spells, life drain, can animate blood as weapons
- _Special:_ Stored blood vials grant emergency healing or power boosts
- _Loot:_ Blood magic grimoire, enchanted blood vials, rare alchemical components

### Shadow Isles-Touched

**Mistwraith** (Spectral, CR 2) _Environment: Ancient battlefields, cursed sites, places of great death_ Incorporeal shadowy figure wrapped in Black Mist. Vaguely humanoid but features indistinct. Phases through solid matter.

- _Behavior:_ Drawn to living beings, drains warmth and vitality, pack hunters
- _Combat:_ Incorporeal (resistant to non-magical physical damage), life drain attacks
- _Special:_ Becomes stronger for each other Mistwraith nearby
- _Loot:_ None (dissipates), but may guard treasure where they spawned

**Spectral Rider** (Mounted Wraith, CR 3) _Environment: Ancient roads, battlefields, paths where soldiers died_ Ghost of cavalryman on spectral horse. Semi-transparent, glowing eyes, endless patrol.

- _Behavior:_ Eternally guards/patrols their post, attacks intruders, may be reasoned with if approached correctly
- _Combat:_ Mounted charge attacks, ethereal weapons, can phase through obstacles
- _Special:_ Cannot leave their "assigned" area (usually patrol route or battlefield)
- _Loot:_ Ancient weapons/armor where they died (must find corpse location)

**Wraithcaller** (Summoner, CR 4) _Environment: Shadow Isles-corrupted areas, sites of dark rituals_ Living necromancer corrupted by Black Mist. Half-alive, serves the Ruination. Summons lesser wraiths.

- _Behavior:_ Tactical, stays back while minions attack, flees if overwhelmed
- _Combat:_ Summons 1d4 Mistwraiths per round (max limit), minor necromantic spells
- _Special:_ If killed, all summoned wraiths dissipate
- _Loot:_ Shadow Isles artifacts, necromantic texts, cursed jewelry

### Shuriman Creatures (Ancient Tunnels)

**Sand Scarab, Giant** (Swarm, CR 1) _Environment: Ancient Shuriman tunnels, sandy caverns_ Massive scarab beetles size of large dogs. Chitinous golden-brown carapaces, aggressive when disturbed. Usually in groups of 3-5.

- _Behavior:_ Territorial, attack en masse if nest threatened
- _Combat:_ Hardened carapace (high AC), powerful mandibles, burrow to ambush
- _Loot:_ Scarab carapace (shield material), desert beetle meat (rations)

**Sandswimmer, Juvenile** (Ambush Predator, CR 2) _Environment: Sandy areas of ancient tunnels_ Smaller version of massive desert creature. Serpentine body with paddle-like fins, "swims" through packed sand/loose stone. 10-15 feet long.

- _Behavior:_ Ambush predator, senses vibrations, strikes from below
- _Combat:_ Burrow speed, surprise attacks, constriction/bite
- _Special:_ Creates tremors before emerging (Perception check for warning)
- _Loot:_ Sandswimmer hide (water-resistant leather)

**Sandstone Charger** (Golem, CR 3) _Environment: Ancient Shuriman ruins, inactive until disturbed_ Ancient stone construct, humanoid but weathered. Created to guard important locations. Covered in faded Shuriman glyphs.

- _Behavior:_ Defensive, activates when treasures threatened, follows simple commands
- _Combat:_ High AC and HP, powerful melee attacks, immune to many conditions
- _Special:_ Can be deactivated with proper Shuriman command words (History/Investigation check)
- _Loot:_ Shuriman stone (crafting), ancient coins, sometimes guards treasure

**Dormun Grub** (Cave Dweller, CR 2) _Environment: Deep caverns, areas with underground water_ Juvenile stage of massive desert creature. Size of horse, blind, covered in chitinous plates. Senses through vibration.

- _Behavior:_ Blind but sensitive to movement, territorial of water sources
- _Combat:_ Tough armor, powerful crushing bite, resistant to physical damage
- _Special:_ Drawn to water sources, can be lured/avoided using this
- _Loot:_ Dormun plate (excellent shield material), water-finding organs (alchemical)

### Tunnel Horrors

**Arachnoid Horror** (Giant Spider, CR 3) _Environment: Dark tunnels, abandoned ruins, anywhere dark and enclosed_ Massive mutated spider, size of small horse. Shadow Isles corruption enhanced normal spider to monstrous size. Eight gleaming eyes, dripping fangs.

- _Behavior:_ Ambush predator, creates web traps, territorial
- _Combat:_ Web attacks (restrain), poisonous bite, wall-climbing
- _Special:_ Lair usually contains cocooned victims (some may still be alive)
- _Loot:_ Spider silk (valuable), venom glands (poison ingredient), whatever victims had

**Frenzied Skitterer** (Fast Spider, CR 1) _Environment: Tunnels, sewers, ruins - anywhere Arachnoid Horrors live_ Man-sized spider, unnaturally fast. Offspring or lesser variant of Arachnoid Horror.

- _Behavior:_ Hit-and-run attacks, works in packs, harries prey to exhaustion
- _Combat:_ Very high speed, weak bite, pack tactics, flee if severely injured
- _Loot:_ Minor spider parts

**Doombeast** (Life-Drainer, CR 2) _Environment: Shadow Isles-touched areas, cursed tunnels_ Bat-winged creature about size of large dog. Shadow Isles corruption manifested as hunting beast. Glowing eyes, emits darkness aura.

- _Behavior:_ Hunts living creatures to drain life force, prefers weak/isolated targets
- _Combat:_ Flying, life drain attacks (heals self), creates darkness around itself
- _Special:_ Drawn to bright light sources (attacks torches/magic lights)
- _Loot:_ Void-touched membrane (wings), shadow essence

**Stygian Onlooker** (Undead Soldier, CR 2) _Environment: Ancient battlefields, military crypts, places where soldiers died en masse_ Reanimated soldier from long-ago battles. Still wears ancient armor, wielding corroded weapons. Empty eye sockets glow with cold light.

- _Behavior:_ Becomes stronger as allies fall (grows more aggressive with each death nearby)
- _Combat:_ Basic soldier skills, gets combat bonuses when allies die
- _Special:_ "Feeds" on death nearby, gaining power from fallen comrades
- _Loot:_ Ancient military equipment, old coins, historical artifacts

### Elite/Boss Variants

**Spectral Matron** (Powerful Wraith, CR 5) _Environment: Major Shadow Isles-corrupted sites, ancient noble crypts_ Powerful feminine wraith, appears as noble lady in flowing translucent gown. Commands lesser spirits. Face beautiful but eyes empty and cold.

- _Behavior:_ Regal, commands lesser undead, may parley if approached with respect
- _Combat:_ Summons spirits, life drain, spectral weapons, phase through walls
- _Special:_ Can possess corpses temporarily to communicate, guards specific area/treasure
- _Loot:_ Ancient noble jewelry, Shadow Isles artifacts, necromantic focus

**Alpha Sandswimmer** (Large Predator, CR 4) _Environment: Sandy tunnel areas with room to maneuver_ Adult sandswimmer that dwells in ancient tunnel system. 25-30 feet long, scarred from many hunts.

- _Behavior:_ Apex predator of tunnel system, highly territorial
- _Combat:_ All juvenile abilities enhanced, can swallow medium creatures whole
- _Special:_ Creates sandstorm effect when breaching (obscures vision)
- _Loot:_ Premium sandswimmer hide, massive teeth (weapons), sometimes has treasure in gullet

**Crimson Awakener** (Blood Mage Master, CR 5) _Environment: Hidden Crimson Circle temples, usually city underbelly_ High-ranking blood mage of Vladimir's circle. Pale as death, eyes bloodshot, surrounded by floating blood orbs.

- _Behavior:_ Tactical mastermind, uses followers as resources, escapes if clearly losing
- _Combat:_ Advanced blood magic, animates blood as weapons, life drain, blood healing
- _Special:_ Can sacrifice cultist followers for massive power boost
- _Loot:_ Master blood magic tome, enchanted blood chalice, rare blood ritual components

**Carnage Demon** (Side Quest Boss, CR 6) _Environment: Wilderness, terrorizing countryside near Quathala_ Massive hulking creature, 10ft tall, bulging muscles. NOT actually a demon—failed Noxian military experiment or trauma-twisted war veteran transformed by dark magic and rage. Dark red skin, burning eyes, endless fury.

- _Behavior:_ Mindlessly violent, attacks anything encountered, no tactics just rage
- _Combat:_ Devastating physical attacks, high HP, resistant to CC, weak to cold/psychic
- _Special:_ Rage state—gets stronger as injured (like berserker)
- _Loot:_ 500gp bounty from Roderick, experimental Noxian alchemical compounds, enchanted rage-touched items
