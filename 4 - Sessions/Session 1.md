## SCENE 1: Roderick Meeting

### Setting

Duke's Manor main hall. The large oaken table (Borric's old war table) dominates the center, covered with an ornate runner. Weapons on walls with flower arrangements beneath them. Architectural drawings and marble samples scattered on side tables. Windows overlook the river on both sides.

Roderick sits at the head of the table. Matthias stands nearby. Other applicants are present - various mercenary types, soldiers, adventurers.

---

### Opening

**As party enters with the other applicants:**

A servant announces them. Roderick looks up from examining architectural drawings spread before him.

> **RODERICK:** "Ah! The adventurers. Please, come forward, all of you."

Servants gesture everyone toward chairs around the table. Before anyone can properly introduce themselves or bow:

> **RODERICK:** "I don't stand on formality when I dine with those who serve the domain. Matthias, did we order enough wine? I think we should have more wine. And those pastries from that baker on Fifth Street - the ones with the honey glaze? We must have those."

**Food begins arriving** - an impressive spread being laid out by servants. Roderick settles into his chair, gesturing for everyone to sit.

> **RODERICK:** "Please, eat. Tell me—" [his eyes scan the table, landing on Marduun] "—you! A Shuriman, yes? I've read about your capital. A magnificent city bathed in gold, with architecture reaching toward the heavens. How tragic that such beauty was lost to the sands..."

[Leans forward with genuine interest]

> **RODERICK:** "But tell me, in your travels, what have you observed? Do citizens of other cities respond better to symmetrical gardens—orderly, controlled, everything in its proper place—or natural, flowing arrangements that mimic the wild? I've been debating the design for Quathala's street gardens for weeks now, and I simply cannot decide."

**After Marduun responds, Roderick becomes animated:**

> **RODERICK:** [nodding enthusiastically or thoughtfully, depending on answer] "Yes, yes! Exactly what I've been thinking! You see, Matthias doesn't understand but the gardens must complement the marble facades. The white-gray stone from Stonewall, it catches the light in such a way that..."

[He continues talking about water features, plant selection, sight lines etc.]

---

### Bennar's Moment

**During a natural lull in the conversation, Roderick's attention shifts:**

> **RODERICK:** [stops mid-thought, attention shifting] "Wait. Is that—are you a musician?"

His whole manner changes - becomes focused, almost childlike in his interest.

> **RODERICK:** "Music. That's civilization, right there. Not just war war war, but beauty made tangible. Art that moves the soul."

[Expression blanks for a moment]

[Refocuses on Bennar]

> **RODERICK:** "Play something. Just a few bars. Anything."

**After Bennar plays** (regardless of quality):

> **RODERICK:** [visibly moved] "Magnificent. That's what I'm trying to save in Quathala. That feeling."

---

### Deflecting Stonewall

**If any applicant tries to ask about the job:**

> **RODERICK:** [waves hand dismissively] "Yes, yes, we'll get to that. But first—" [gestures toward windows overlooking the city]"—what do you think of my city?"

**Regardless of their answer:**

> **RODERICK:** "It's not enough. Not yet. I've tried very hard to make Quathala beautiful for those who live here. I would have the day come when all the settlements in my domain are as fine as this, where everywhere the eye travels, there is beauty."

[Picks up a marble sample from the side table]

> **RODERICK:** "You see, where I find brick, I will leave marble. And those who see it will know it for what it is—my legacy. Something that will outlast me. Something my father never understood..."

[Trails off, staring at the marble]

---

### Matthias Redirects

> **MATTHIAS:** [clearing throat gently] "My lord, perhaps we should discuss the matter at Stonewall?"

> **RODERICK:** [blinks, comes back to present] "What? Oh. Yes. Stonewall."

**His face clouds, troubled tone enters his voice:**

> **RODERICK:** "Why does this happen now? There's so much to be done. I can't have... problems... disrupting my plans."

[Stroking his chin, staring at nothing]

> **RODERICK:** "That village exists for ONE purpose - to supply marble from our mountain. The finest quality in the region. Essential for the beautification of Quathala. And now... nothing. Days of silence, then these useless idiots arrive babbling about darkness, creatures, death."

[Returns focus to the group]

> **RODERICK:** "The reports don't even make any sense. Shadows that moved. Things that came from nowhere. The mine itself turning against them. Plague, maybe? Or bandits spreading fear? Or..."

[Looks troubled]

---

### Mukai's Question

> **RODERICK:** [looking at Mukai] "You. You look like you know something about survival in harsh conditions. Tell me - if you heard an entire village went silent, and then refugees fled with stories of monsters, what would be your first instinct about what really happened?"

**After Mukai gives his answer:**

> **RODERICK:** [nodding] "Logical. Very logical. A practical mind."

> **RODERICK:** "So tell me. If there have been as many deaths as the refugees claim, considering how terrified these survivors are, what does that tell you about what we're facing?"

**After they give logical analysis:**

> **RODERICK:** [slaps table] "A good exercise in logic! Exactly!"

---

### The Instant Decision

> **RODERICK:** [suddenly standing up] "Perfect! You [Marduun], you [Mukai], And you [Rha] - I've never seen anything quite like you. Fascinating. You look like you will be usefull in a fight"

[Points to Bennar]

> **RODERICK:** "Ah and the musician ofcourse"

> **MATTHIAS:** [immediately interjecting] "My lord, the musician appears wounded. Perhaps not the most practical choice for a dangerous investigation—"

> **RODERICK:** [dismissive wave] "A few wounds? That just means he's been in a fight and survived. He's will clearly be usefull."

[Gathering his drawings]

> **RODERICK:** "The rest of you, thank you for coming, you are dismissed. Matthias will see you're compensated for your time. As for you four—" [to the chosen party] "—Matthias will provide all the details. I have important business regarding the canal system to attend to."

**He exits quickly, already muttering to himself about water flow calculations.**

---

**After Roderick leaves, Matthias's demeanor becomes colder and more professional:**

> **MATTHIAS:** [looking over the party with clear disappointment] "Well. The Duke has made his choice."

[Sighs]

> **MATTHIAS:** "Here are your instructions. Stonewall lies four days' journey south along the mountain road. The situation is... unclear. The village has gone silent. Refugees speak of creatures and darkness, though whether this is plague-induced madness, bandits, or something else remains to be seen."

> **MATTHIAS:** "The Duke promises substantial compensation upon successful completion - land, title, a place in his vision for the domain. The specifics will depend on your success."

[Looks at the party's equipment and condition, lips thinning]

> **MATTHIAS:** "You'll need supplies. Here is twenty gold pieces as an advance. I suggest you use it wisely."

[His tone makes it clear this is more than he thinks they deserve]

> **MATTHIAS:** "A handful of refugees made it here they are housed in the crafter's district. If you wish to gather information before proceeding, they may have useful intelligence."

[Straightens]

> **MATTHIAS:** "The Duke expects results. Find out what has become of the villagers and how many survived. determine what happened to the village, and secure the marble mine if possible. Any questions?"


## SCENE 2: Adventuring Day

### ENCOUNTER 1: The Survivors (Mid-Morning, Day 3 or 4)

Around a bend in the mountain road, five figures stumble into view, moving toward Quathala. They're filthy, exhausted, and one man is limping badly with bloody claw marks torn through his leg. A woman carries a crying child.

**The Group:**

- **Goram** (50s, miner) - leg wound, losing blood
- **Dessa** (30s, farmer's wife) - carrying young son (5 years old)
- **Petyr** (20s, blacksmith's apprentice) - uninjured but shaken
- **Old Maris** (60s, weaver) - exhausted, barely standing
- Young boy **Jem** (5) - Dessa's son, terrified

They left Stonewall 5-6 days ago, part of a group that couldn't wait for help. Made slow progress. Camped last night in what seemed like a safe clearing off the road.

**What They Know:**

- "Monsters came at dawn. Screaming, claws everywhere. Rella and Old Toben—they didn't make it."
- "We ran. Kept running. Don't know if those things are still following."
- **About Stonewall:** "There's a camp. Hour north of the village. Maybe fifteen people still there—too scared or too weak to leave. We tried to convince them..."
- Goram needs medical attention (DC 12 Medicine check to stabilize, or he'll worsen)

**Their campsite is about 2 hours back down the road** - they've been walking since dawn.

Party can give them food/water/supplies to help them reach Quathala, or leave them to their fate.

---

### ENCOUNTER 2: The Nest (2 Hours Later, Early Afternoon)

Following the refugees' directions or simple backtracking, you find where they camped. The clearing lies about 200 feet off the main road, hidden by dense trees.

**What's Obvious:**

- Bloody scene in a small clearing
- Two bodies, badly mauled
- Scattered supplies and belongings
- Scorch marks and claw tracks everywhere
- The smell of blood and something else—musky, reptilian

**The Raptors:**

- **3 Raptors** lounging near the bodies, one still feeding
- **1 Crimson Raptor** (larger, alpha) perched on a rock near the nest
- They notice the party at 60 feet
- Crimson Raptor screeches warning; pack goes alert

**DC 13 Perception Check (or Passive Perception 13):**

- Spots the nest tucked against the rocks 40 feet from the campsite
- Six large eggs, pale blue with red veining
- This wasn't random hunting—this was territorial defense

**Recoverable:**

- Refugees' supplies: 2 days rations, waterskins, rope, 8 GP
- Bodies can be buried (refugees would appreciate this if party mentions it later)
- Raptor eggs (6 total) - worth 25 GP each in the right market, or could be hatched
