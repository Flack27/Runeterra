### Description

A large stone compound that once housed over 500 soldiers now stands eerily vacant. Training yards are overgrown with weeds, barracks doors hang open, and equipment racks sit empty. A few scavenged practice dummies lie broken in the courtyard. The building itself is solid - built to last - but the abandonment is a stark visual reminder of Roderick's failure.

### Current State

- Main gates unlocked and unguarded
- Windows dark, some shutters banging in wind
- Occasional homeless person sheltering in abandoned barracks

### NPCs

**Old Garrett** (Veteran, 60s, male, Noxian)

- **Appearance**: Weathered face with white beard, missing three fingers on left hand, wears faded military uniform (still pressed daily), stands with slight limp
- Former quartermaster who was left in charge
- Lives in old quartermaster's office
- Keeps a few rooms "ready" in case soldiers return
- **Traits**: Stubborn, nostalgic, bitter about abandonment
- **Speech**: Grumbles, refers to "the old days," calls current situation "a damn shame"
