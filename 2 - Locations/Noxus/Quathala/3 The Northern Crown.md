### Description

Once an upscale inn for traveling merchants, now clearly struggling. The building is well-maintained but the common room sits mostly empty. Fine wooden furniture seems too elegant for the few patrons present. The owner maintains standards despite declining business. Rooms are clean but expensive.

### Current State

- 1-2 wealthy travelers staying at any time
- Common room empty except during evening meal
- Prices remain high (owner refuses to "lower standards")
- Food is actually quite good

### NPCs

**Leonara Brightwater** (Owner, 40s, female, Noxian)

- **Appearance**: Tall and elegant with graying auburn hair in elaborate updo, expensive but practical dress, jewelry that's real but modest, maintains perfect posture, aging beauty with laugh lines and worry creases
- Former merchant herself, retired to run "respectable establishment"
- Proud, refuses to admit business is failing
- Maintains appearance of success
- **Traits**: Dignified, slightly desperate, passive-aggressive about money
- **Speech**: Formal, "One simply cannot," emphasizes quality over everything
- **Notable**: Will gossip about noble affairs if treated with respect

### Prices

- Room: 1 GP per night (includes breakfast)
- Meal: 5 SP (actually worth it - good food)
- Bath: 2 SP
