### Description

A large open square with permanent stalls and small shops around the perimeter. Once the bustling heart of Quathala's commerce, now struggling with reduced foot traffic. Many stalls are empty or only open part-time. The merchants who remain are determined survivors, calling out to any potential customer. Morning is busiest; afternoons are quiet.

### Current State

- About 60% of stalls operating
- Prices negotiable (merchants desperate for sales)
- Quality varies wildly

### Market NPCs & Stalls

**Greta Ironkettle** (Produce Stall, 50s, female, Noxian)

- **Appearance**: Broad-shouldered farm woman, sun-darkened skin, gray hair in practical bun, permanent squint from outdoor work

### Market Rumors & Atmosphere

**Morning (Busiest)**

- Merchants calling out wares
- Housewives haggling
- Children running between stalls

**Afternoon (Quiet)**

- Most customers gone
- Merchants pack up early
- Good time for private conversations

**Conversations Overheard**:

- "Can't afford Duke's taxes AND food"
- "Roads ain't safe - three merchant caravans hit last month"
- "Heard Stonewall went silent - plague maybe?"
- "That marble project costs more per day than my farm makes in a year"
