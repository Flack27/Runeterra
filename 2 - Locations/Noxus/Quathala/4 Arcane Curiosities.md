## Description

A substantial three-story building standing proudly on its own corner lot in the merchant district. The stone structure is adorned with permanently enchanted lanterns that glow with soft blue light, and a large wooden sign featuring an intricate glowing rune hangs above the heavy oak door.

**Ground Floor**: Spacious main sales area with high ceilings and shelves reaching two stories. Glass cases display valuable components. Spiral staircase leads up. Air hums with residual magic.

**Second Floor**: Aldric's workshop - workbenches, books, half-finished projects. Where custom orders are prepared.

**Third Floor**: Aldric's quarters and extensive magical library.

**Basement**: Climate-controlled storage for sensitive components and vault.

### Current State

- Well-stocked but inventory decreased from pre-war levels
- Prices slightly higher (difficult to restock from Ionia)
- Still primary magical supplier for the region
- Fewer customers but remains profitable

### NPCs

**Aldric Thornweave** (Owner, 30s, male, Noxian with Ionian education)

- **Appearance**: Thin with pale skin from indoor life, long dark hair tied messily, spectacles perched on nose, ink-stained fingers, robes dusted with component residue, tattoo of arcane symbols on forearm (from Ionian studies)
- Quiet scholar more interested in research than sales
- Studied briefly in Ionia before the war, returned when conflict started
- Knows a lot about local magical theory
- **Traits**: Distracted, knowledgeable, apologetic about prices
- **Speech**: Trails off mid-sentence, "Hmm, where did I...," mutters about his research, occasionally uses Ionian magical terms
- **Notable**: Will trade information for interesting magical items or phenomena

 **Mira Ashvale** (Shop Manager, 40s, female, Noxian)

- **Appearance**: Iron-gray hair in tight bun, sharp hazel eyes, simple quality robes in dark blue, reading glasses on chain, carries ledger and quill, efficient movements
- No magical talent but encyclopedic knowledge of components
- Actually runs day-to-day business while Aldric researches
- Worked here 15 years, keeps meticulous inventory
- **Traits**: Organized, practical, patient, business-minded, protective of Aldric
- **Speech**: Clear and direct, "Let me check our inventory," calls Aldric "Master Thornweave" to customers, firm on prices
- **Notable**: Handles ground floor sales and inventory, knows value of everything, won't haggle much

 **Jorik Stone** (Apprentice, 18, male, Noxian)

- **Appearance**: Gangly teenager still growing into height, messy brown hair, simple apprentice robes (dark green, slightly too short), eager expression, ink smudges on face
- Apprentice for two years, minor magical talent
- Handles stock, cleaning, basic customer service
- **Traits**: Enthusiastic, eager to help, occasionally clumsy, hero-worships Aldric
- **Speech**: Fast talker when excited, "I can help you with that!", "Master Thornweave is brilliant!", apologizes for minor things
- **Notable**: Very impressed by adventurers, might knock something over if nervous, fetches Aldric or Mira when needed
