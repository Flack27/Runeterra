### Description

The finest inn in Quathala's wealthy district. White marble columns frame the entrance (imported at great expense from Stonewall ironically). Inside, polished wood floors, velvet curtains, and brass fixtures create an atmosphere of refined luxury. Clientele are nobles, wealthy merchants, and important visitors. Common folk wouldn't dream of entering.

### Current State

- Well-maintained despite economic downturn
- Protected by Duke's favor (no tax increases here)
- Catering to fewer but wealthier guests

### NPCs

**Corvin Brightmantle** (Proprietor, 50s, male, Noxian nobility - minor house)

- **Appearance**: Impeccably dressed in expensive merchant's clothes, oiled black hair with silver streaks, thin mustache waxed to points, soft hands with manicured nails
- Obsequious to nobles, dismissive of commoners
- From minor noble family that lost status, rebuilt through business
- **Traits**: Snobbish, calculating, excellent memory
- **Speech**: Formal, flowery language, "One would be delighted to..." judges people instantly
- **Notable**: Will refuse service to "unsuitable" guests

**Servina** (Head Server, 30s, female, Noxian)

- **Appearance**: Professional and neat, dark hair in tight bun, simple but quality server's uniform, graceful movements, observant dark eyes, poker face perfected over years
- Always seems to appear exactly when needed
- Efficient, professional, sees everything
- Kind to staff, neutral to guests
- **Traits**: Observant, efficient, discretely helpful
- **Speech**: Professional, "Right away, ser/milady," barely shows emotion
