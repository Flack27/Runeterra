### Description

The industrial heart of Quathala - smithies, carpenter shops, tanneries, and workshops cluster together. Sounds of hammering, sawing, and industry fill the air along with smoke and less pleasant smells. Craftsmen work hard but business is slow. Many take commissions for the marble project just to survive, though they'd rather do real work.

### Current State

- About 70% of workshops still operating
- Those remaining work overtime on Roderick's projects
- Resentment toward marble work
- Apprentices being sent away (can't afford to train them)

### Workshop NPCs

**Gilda Ironhammer** (Smith, 40s, female, Noxian - claims distant Freljordian ancestry)

- **Appearance**: Muscular and stocky at 5'4", braided black hair with iron rings woven through, burn scars on massive forearms, leather apron permanently scorched, soot-stained face
- Best smith in Quathala
- Forced to make decorative ironwork for marble buildings
- Would rather make weapons/armor/tools (did during the war)
- War veteran (served in Ionian campaigns as military smith)
- **Traits**: Gruff, proud of craft, hates wasted talent, respects strength
- **Speech**: Direct, curses creatively, "This is beneath my skill," military metaphors, "Back in Ionia, we needed REAL iron work"
- **Services**:
    - Can craft weapons/armor to order (takes 1 week, normal prices)
    - Currently too busy with Duke's work (might be convinced if party helps her or shows martial prowess)

---

**Carpenter Jonus Oakhand** (Carpenter, 50s, male, Noxian)

- **Appearance**: Tall and lean with sawdust perpetually in thinning brown hair, gentle eyes behind protective goggles pushed up on forehead, scarred hands from decades of work
- Builds scaffolding for marble project
- Family business for three generations
- Can't afford to refuse work (has two daughters to support)
- Misses building real furniture and houses
- **Traits**: Melancholic, skilled, quietly bitter, resigned
- **Speech**: Slow and careful, "It's honest work I suppose," sighs often, "My grandfather would weep..."

---

**Old Marcus Tanner** (Tanner, 60s, male, Noxian)

- **Appearance**: Weathered face like old leather (fitting), bald head with liver spots, permanently hunched from decades of work, strong despite age, stained hands and apron
- Process leather and hides
- Business declining (fewer hunters due to unsafe roads, less trade)
- Stubborn about maintaining quality despite economic pressure
- **Traits**: Stubborn, traditional, practical, smell of tannery follows him
- **Speech**: Grumbles, "Made it this way for forty years," resistant to change, "Quality over speed"

### Quarter Atmosphere

**During Work Hours**:

- Loud (hammering, sawing, industrial noise)
- Smoky (forges and kilns)
- Busy but stressed workers
- Material deliveries coming and going

**After Hours**:

- Complaints about marble project
- Stories from Ionian war (from veterans)
- Gossip about Duke's mismanagement
