### Description

The Voss family estate dominates a small island in the middle of the river that flows through Quathala's heart. The manor covers the entire island, connected to the city by three stone bridges. The building itself is solid stone construction - military practicality reflecting Borric's values as a Noxian general. However, Roderick has begun "improvements": marble is being added to the facade, ornamental gardens planted along the riverbanks, and decorative elements installed that clash with the original austere design. The result is jarring - like putting lace on armor.

The island grounds are well-maintained but clearly over-staffed (Roderick employs more people than needed). Guards at each bridge look bored rather than alert - a stark contrast to the disciplined soldiers who served under Borric. The sound of the river flowing around the island creates a constant ambient backdrop.

### Current State

- Guards present at all three bridges but unmotivated (12 total, rotating shifts)
- Marble renovation 30% complete on exterior
- Staff overpaid but underutilized
- Portraits of Borric in military dress watch disapprovingly throughout
- Gardens along riverbank consuming significant maintenance budget

### Main Hall

Where Roderick receives visitors. Originally designed for military briefings, now cluttered with decorative elements:

- Large oaken table (Borric's war table) with ornate runner (Roderick's)
- Weapons on walls (Borric's trophies from campaigns) with flower arrangements beneath them (Roderick's touch)
- New paintings of landscapes covering military efficiency charts
- Windows overlook the river on both sides

### Manor NPCs

**Captain Helena Vortis** (Guard Captain, 35, female, Noxian)

- **Appearance**: Military bearing with athletic build, dark hair in practical bun, weathered face from years of outdoor patrols, prominent scar across left eyebrow (from Ionian campaign)
- Competent soldier stuck with ceremonial duty
- Served under Borric Voss in Ionia, transferred here out of loyalty to the family
- Protective of Duke despite disagreeing with his choices (honors his father's memory)
- **Traits**: Professional, loyal but frustrated, military bearing, quiet disappointment
- **Speech**: Crisp, formal, "Sir" and "Ma'am," occasional exasperation, military terminology
- **Notable**: Will escort party to Roderick, assesses them as potential threats/assets with veteran's eye

**Cornelius Graymark** (Head Butler, 60s, male, Noxian)

- **Appearance**: Impeccably dressed in formal servant's attire, straight posture despite age, thinning white hair combed perfectly, lined face with perpetually disapproving expression
- Served Borric Voss for 30 years, now serves Roderick
- Disapproves of changes but maintains absolute loyalty to the Voss family
- Actually manages the household staff and manor operations
- **Traits**: Formal, nostalgic, subtle judgment, unfailingly professional
- **Speech**: "In Lord Borric's time...," sighs frequently when Roderick isn't listening, maintains perfect appearances, "Very good, my lord" (even when it isn't)
