### Description

A weathered two-story building in the workers' district with faded paint and a crooked sign showing a foaming mug. Inside, rough wooden tables are filled with day laborers, dock workers, and common folk. The air smells of cheap ale, sweat, and fried fish. A few oil lamps provide dim lighting. This is where the common people gather to complain.

### Current State

- Packed during evenings (workers finishing shifts)
- Quiet during day except for unemployed regulars
- Food is cheap but filling
- Ale is watered down but affordable (2 copper per mug)

### NPCs

**"Pops" Hendrik Voss** (Innkeeper, 50s, male, Noxian)

- **Appearance**: Barrel-chested with massive forearms, bald with bushy gray eyebrows, permanent scowl, stained apron, roll of fat over belt
- No relation to Duke Roderick despite shared surname
- Been running this place for 30 years
- Knows everyone's business but keeps quiet about most of it
- **Traits**: Gruff exterior, soft heart, protective of regulars
- **Speech**: Short sentences, grunts a lot, "Ayep" and "Nope" are favorites
- **Notable**: Will talk freely about common complaints after a few drinks

**Young Tam** (Server, 16, male, Noxian)

- **Appearance**: Lanky teenage boy with too-big ears, messy brown hair, gap-toothed smile, patched clothes, moves with nervous energy
- Pops' grandson, works for room and board
- Dreams of joining the military
- Listens to everything, remembers everything
- **Traits**: Curious, hero-worship of soldiers, eager to help
- **Speech**: Fast talker, interrupts himself, "Did you hear about—"
