### Description

Roderick's grand vision partially realized. Several blocks of buildings now have marble facades, though the work is clearly incomplete. Construction continues on some buildings while others stand half-finished, wrapped in scaffolding. Ornamental gardens line streets, lovingly maintained despite seeming out of place. The canal project is visible - partially dug trenches that serve no current purpose. It's beautiful but impractical, and locals resent it.

### Current State

- Active construction (workers during day)
- Marble dust everywhere
- Streets sometimes blocked by materials
- Gardens maintained by dedicated (taxpayer-funded) staff

### NPCs

**Foreman Deckard Stone** (Construction Lead, 40s, male, Noxian)

- **Appearance**: Powerfully built with sun-darkened skin, short-cropped graying hair, perpetual marble dust in beard stubble, calloused hands, work-worn clothes
- Competent builder frustrated by impractical orders
- Knows the project wastes money but needs the work to feed his family
- **Traits**: Practical, frustrated, professional, protective of workers
- **Speech**: Technical jargon, heavy sighs, "Duke wants it this way," curses under breath
- **Notable**: Will complain to sympathetic ears about waste, especially if they buy him a drink

### What Players See

- Marble facades on 1 in 4 buildings
- Partially dug canals going nowhere
- Workers clearly uncertain about purpose
- Beautiful but impractical gardens (some with Ionian-inspired elements)
- **Cost**: Visible signs everywhere - "Funded by Ducal Treasury"

### Reactions from Locals

- Resentment: "Marble don't fill my belly"
- Confusion: "What's them ditches for?"
- Anger: "My taxes paid for THAT?"
- Fear of complaining: "Duke's project, watch your tongue"
