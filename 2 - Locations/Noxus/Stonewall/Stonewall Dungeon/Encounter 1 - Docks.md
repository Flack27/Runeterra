**Location:** The river docks near Amos's burning ship (central area)

**Setup:** As the party arrives from the bay, they see Amos Trask desperately fighting off Voidlings on the dock near his flaming vessel. Crates of black powder are still aboard—the ship could explode at any moment. Amos is wounded, his crew's bodies scattered on the dock.

**Creatures:**

- 4 Voidlings (CR 1/4 each) swarming around Amos
- 1 Voidmantle (CR 1/2) clinging to a dock piling, waiting to drop on anyone approaching

**Environmental Hazards:**

- Burning ship threatens explosion (party has ~5 rounds before it blows)
- Loose dock planks—DEX saves to avoid stumbling
- Smoke provides light obscurement

**Interesting Moments:**

- Amos shouts warnings in colorful Bilgewater curses
- A Voidling is dragging a dead crewman toward the water—do they stop it?
- The explosion can be used tactically if the party is creative (luring Voidlings aboard)

**After Combat:**

- Amos explains he arrived hours ago during the storm, found the village like this
- His crew didn't last ten minutes on the docks
- He can describe what he saw: creatures carrying bodies _toward the mountain_
