The dock boards groan under your feet as you approach. The ship burns slowly, flames licking up the mainmast. Smoke churns across the pier in thick coils, carrying the smell of tar and something worse. Bodies lie scattered on the dock planks. Sailors.

Then you hear it—muffled, desperate, coming from the ship itself: "Help! For the love of the Bearded Lady, is someone there? HELP ME!"

The voice comes from the captain's cabin at the stern. The door is barricaded from the inside.

Between you and that cabin: four dog-sized creatures with pale violet-white flesh and chitinous legs pick through the bodies. They have no eyes, but their heads turn toward your direction.

**Creatures:**

- 4 Voidlings (CR 1/4 each)
- 1 Voidmantle (CR 1/2) clinging to a dock piling beneath the pier, waiting to strike

**The Situation:**

- Amos Trask is barricaded in the captain's cabin
- The ship is burning but not yet critical

**After Combat Begins (Round 2 or 3):** Once combat is engaged and the party is clearly fighting, Amos bursts from the cabin with a cutlass:

_"The powder stores! There's black powder in the hold—if the fire reaches it, we're all dead! We need to move, NOW!"_

**Tactical Options:**

- Party can use the black powder threat to their advantage (luring Voidlings aboard)
- The Voidmantle under the pier ambushes anyone who approaches the water's edge

**After Combat:** Amos Trask catches his breath, eyes haunted:

_"Arrived in a storm. Ship was barely holding together. Thought this was salvation—a quiet little mining village, what could go wrong?"_

He spits.

_"Crew didn't last ten minutes once we docked. Those things came out of nowhere. Dragged them off toward the mountain."_

If asked what he saw: _"The flying ones. Carrying bodies like... like they were harvesting. All heading the same direction. Toward the mine, or maybe past it. Something up there is hungry."_
