The inn still stand moderately well, only 2 walls are partially collapsed, the sign still swings on rusted chains—a pickaxe crossed with a tankard.

As you approach, you see it.

A creature resembling some sort of jellyfish drifts out from the inn's entrance, its translucent purple membrane pulsing with sickly bioluminescence. Wrapped in its tentacles, held upright like a hanged man, is the body of a heavyset man in a stained apron. His arms dangling, feet dragging across the cobblestones as the creature carries him toward the mountain.

The creature hasn't noticed you yet. It moves with terrible purpose, focused entirely on its cargo.

**If They Attack the Voidmantle:** It will drop the body and attempt to flee toward the mountain. It has no interest in fighting—only in completing its task. If prevented from escaping, it fights desperately but without tactics.

- 1 [Voidmantle](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194959-darkmantle) (CR 1/2)
- 4 [Voidlings](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5974289-voidling) (CR 1/4) will jump in after 1 round.

**Inside the Inn:**

The common room tells the story of a week of survival turned to tragedy.

Tables have been pushed against windows. Chairs stacked to form barricades. The bar has been stripped—bottles emptied, food stores ransacked. Someone lived here, hiding, rationing, waiting for rescue that never came.

Dark stains mark the floorboards near the kitchen door—fresh blood, still tacky. A trail of it leads from the cellar trapdoor toward the front entrance. The innkeeper made it almost to the door before they caught him.

**After Combat: The Cellar**

Sobbing.

**In the Cellar**

A rectangle of candlelight spills up from the cellar. The smell of root vegetables, damp stone, and fear. And there, at the bottom of the wooden steps, a young woman looks up.

First: _hope_. 

Then: _confusion_. 

_"You're not—where's my father? Have you seen him? He went out this morning, he said he'd be right back"_

**Mira Ashwood:**

- Age 23, innkeeper's daughter
- Hair tangled and unwashed
- Clothes worn and dirty
- Dehydrated, malnourished, exhausted

**The Conversation:**

If the party tells her gently: She crumbles. Sits down on the cellar steps and just... stops. Stares at nothing. The tears come silently now.

_"I told him not to go. I told him we could wait another day. I told him..."_

**What Mira Knows:**

- The attack came at night, over a week ago
- Her father barricaded them in the cellar immediately
- They heard the screaming, the breaking glass, the chittering
- For days, the sounds continued—creatures moving through the village, dragging things
- It got quieter over the past few days, but never silent
