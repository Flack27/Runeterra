This chamber was once a library.

Stone shelves line every wall, carved directly into the rock, rising from floor to ceiling. Hundreds of them. Thousands of books once rested here.

Now there is only dust.

Stone reading tables occupy the center of this room, their surfaces carved with grooves meant to hold books open.

The corruption is light here, creeping in from the connecting passages. Purple tendrils wind along the upper shelves like searching fingers, as if the growth itself is looking for something worth consuming and finding nothing.

**Encounter:** 2 Voidlings lurk among the empty shelves, feeding on whatever residual energy remains. 

**Loot:**
- **Locked Chest** (DC 12 to pick, DC 15 to force): Contains items Keth'vyss had no use for but couldn't bring himself to discard:
    - 85 gold pieces in Half Securis (shuriman currency)
    - A silver letter opener shaped like a feather (25 gp, decorative)


**The Study**

This smaller chamber adjoins the library—a private workspace for focused study.

A stone desk dominates the room, its surface carved with channels and indentations meant to hold inks, styluses, and other writing implements. A high-backed stone chair sits behind it, sized for someone taller than any human. A single book rests on the desk, closed, its leather binding cracked but intact, with a small scarab laying next to it.

**Loot:**

- **The Preserved Journal** — A personal journal written in an ancient script. Most of it is indecipherable without _Comprehend Languages_, but scattered passages are written in an older form of Shuriman that can be partially understood (History DC 15). It speaks of "the spaces between worlds," of "doors that should not be opened," and of growing concerns about something called "the Maw." The final entry reads simply: "They are right to seal me. I cannot stop."
- **Shuriman Scarab Charm** (Uncommon magic item — small carved beetle on leather cord, grants +1 to saving throws)
