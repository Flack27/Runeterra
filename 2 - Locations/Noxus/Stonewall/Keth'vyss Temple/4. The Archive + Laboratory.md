**Laboratory**

Stone tables are arranged in precise patterns, their surfaces stained by substances that have long since dried to unidentifiable residue. Metal apparatus of unknown purpose stands in corners, tarnished green with age.

The corruption is overwhelming here. It covers almost every surface. The floor is carpeted in pulsing organic matter. The walls are more growth than stone. The ceiling _drips_.

One table near the northern wall still holds a set of instruments: bronze tools shaped for precise work, a magnifying lens mounted on an articulated arm, empty vials arranged in careful rows.

**Encounter:** 2 Voidlings lurk here.
