The corridor ends in a chamber that makes you look up.

The walls and ceiling are covered in heavy corruption—purple-black tendrils and pulsing growths coating every surface—through the gaps you can see that covering every inch of the domed surface above, are stars and constellations rendered in exquisite detail.

In the center of the room, a massive bronze apparatus rises from the floor—or it did, once. The mechanism has corroded and collapsed, its intricate gears and lenses scattered across the floor. 

**Encounter:** 2 Voidlings

**Loot:**

- **Salvaged Observation Lenses** (3) — Crystal lenses from the observation device. Worth 30 gp each to artificers or enchanters, or can be used as arcane focus components.
- **Astronomer's Lens** (Uncommon magic item)
- Scattered bronze gears and components (50 gp worth of salvage total)
