A long corridor stretches north from the prayer chamber, lined with alcoves on either side. Five small chambers branch off from this passage, each containing a simple stone table meant for offerings.

The corruption grows heavier as you move north. The southern chambers are relatively clear; the northern ones are thick with purple-black tendrils and pulsing crystalline growths.

**Features:**

- 5 small offering chambers, each with a stone table
- Progression of corruption from light (south) to heavy (north)
- Each chamber may contain remnants of offerings

---

#### Chamber 1 (Southernmost, East Side)

**Atmosphere:** Nearly untouched by corruption. The stone table is bare except for a thin layer of dust.

**Loot:**

- Empty. Whatever was left here has crumbled to nothing.

---

#### Chamber 2 (South-Central, West Side)

**Atmosphere:** Light corruption creeping along the ceiling. The offering table holds a small pile of corroded metal.

**Loot:**

- Corroded coins (Investigation DC 10: 15 Half Securis can be salvaged)
- A tarnished silver holy symbol depicting the sun disc (worth 20 gp)

---

#### Chamber 3 (Central, East Side)

**Atmosphere:** Moderate corruption. Purple tendrils wrap around the offering table's legs.

**Loot:**

- Worthless baubles and trinkets

---

#### Chamber 4 (North-Central, West Side)

**Atmosphere:** Heavy corruption. The growth covers half the floor. The offering table is barely visible beneath pulsing organic matter.

**Loot (requires clearing corruption—DC 10 STR check to pull items free):**

- A gold ring with a small sapphire (50 gp)
- A bone scroll case containing a **Scroll of Shield**

---

#### Chamber 5 (Northernmost, East Side)

**Atmosphere:** Near-overwhelming corruption. The chamber is almost entirely consumed. The offering table is fully engulfed

**Loot (requires clearing corruption):**

- **Ruby Crystal** (Rare magic item)
