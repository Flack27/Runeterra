
A long pool stretches through the center of the room—similar to the entrance hall, but narrower, deeper. The water here is surprisingly clear, fed by some underground spring that still flows after all these years. At the far end of the pool, a statue rises from a raised platform. It depicts a figure in scholarly robes, holding a staff in one hand and an open book in the other. The face is serene, kind—the features of a man who spent his life in pursuit of knowledge. 

Before the statue, offerings have accumulated at the base of the platform. Coins, jewelry, small carved figurines. The corruption has not touched the platform itself, though it creeps along the walls and ceiling of the chamber.

**Encounter:** 3 Voidlings

**Loot (Offering Pile):**

- Ancient coins: 200 Quarter Securis (sp) and 1 Full Securis (100 gp)
- Jewelry: 2 silver rings (10 gp each), a jade pendant (30 gp)
- Small carved figurines depicting the sun disc (6 of them, worth 10 gp each to collectors)
