The passage opens into a grand hall. Four towering statues dominate the chamber, each standing in an alcove along the walls. They depict warriors of impossible stature and bearing. between the statues, small altars have been carved into the walls—simple stone tables where offerings were once left. The double doors at the end of the hall are carved with the great sun disc, have been shattered from within. Stone rubble blocks the passage completely.

Some form of corruption spreads from the gaps in the rubble like a disease through stone. Dark purple-black tendril-like growth crawls across surfaces—somewhere between plant matter and raw flesh, glistening wetly in the dim light.

**Statues**

**Nasus** A towering jackal-headed figure, nearly 12 feet tall, depicted in scholarly repose. One clawed hand rests upon a massive stone tome, the other holds his iconic staff with the curved blade.

**Renekton** A massive crocodilian warrior frozen mid-roar, jaw agape to reveal rows of stone teeth. Unlike Nasus's contemplative pose, this statue radiates pure martial fury - one clawed foot forward, spine arched, both arms raised with curved blades extending from the forearms.

**Xolaani** The most elegant of the four statues, depicting a feminine figure with elongated limbs and graceful, almost dancer-like posture. Her arms are outstretched with palms facing upward, fingers positioned as if conducting an orchestra.

**Setaka** Standing in perfect martial readiness, this statue shows a powerfully built warrior with a leonine helmet - a commanding presence even in stone. She holds a four blade boomerang in her right hand.

**Features:**

- 4 Ascended statues History DC 15: recognizes these as depictions of the legendary Ascended warriors of ancient Shurima—god-warriors who defended the empire in its golden age. 
- Offering altars with remnants of old devotions
- Central pool (the water is dark but not corrupted—merely stagnant. Safe to touch, if unpleasant)
- Collapsed central doors (impassable)
