## Who She Is - Revised

>"I tend civilizations. That's the best way to describe it. I watch them grow, help them when I can—not by solving their problems, but by... lighting the path, you might say. Inspiration during dark times. Hope when they need it most."

>"But some threats are beyond what any mortal society can handle alone. That's when I have to step in directly, even though it goes against everything I'm meant to do."

## Why She Came - Revised with Context

**Building on Option 3 above, when they ask why she intervened:**

"I've only directly intervened once before, millennia ago. A civilization I'd watched grow for centuries—helped through famine, guided through wars, celebrated their triumphs—they made a desperate choice. Opened a door to the void itself."

_[Her voice becomes distant, pained]_

"I tried to stop it. Sealed the breach, but... it cost me. Nearly destroyed me. And the civilization was lost anyway."

_[She refocuses on them]_

"When I sensed Keth'vyss's seal breaking, I couldn't stand by. He studied void portals before his corruption—perfected techniques that could open stable gateways. Combined with Darkin power? He could accomplish what that civilization attempted, but deliberately. Controlled. Sustained."

_[Firmly]_

"I couldn't let it happen again. Even if it meant breaking my own rules about intervention."
