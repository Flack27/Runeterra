
Every surface writhes with corruption—walls pulse, ceiling breathes, floor squelches underfoot. The smell is overwhelming: the same burnt copper, wet stone, cloying sweetness, and the thick stench of death and decay coating your throat with every breath.

At the back of the chamber: a cocoon of crystalline light pressed against the curved wall. The corruption strains toward it but cannot breach. Within, barely visible, is a feminine figure suspended as if sleeping.

In the center bodies lay Fifty, sixty, maybe more. Behind a portal tears at reality—a wound bleeding purple light. Growing from it is a twisted eggshell of pale bone-like matter has cracked open, fragments scattered across the floor. Whatever was inside has hatched.

Behind the pile you hear a wet crunching sound.


**After they walk in**

A creature jumps on the pile of bodies. Humanoid with sleek chitinous plates cover limbs that bend at too many angles. Whip-like appendages extend from its back. Its face is covered in blood still dripping and framed by tendrils that drift upward like hair underwater.

Two or three Voidlings skitter across the walls and ceiling, pausing to watch.

## After Combat - Meeting Her

**Mood:** Calm, practical, genuine concern **Beat:** Offers healing, explains portal urgency. Doesn't push - gives them choice. Patient.

>"Is anyone hurt?"

>"The creature's essence—if you bring it to the barrier, I can use it to sooth your wounds and to seal the portal. Otherwise these creatures will keep pouring out."

If they doubt her or similar:

>"Of course."

Simple agreement. Then:

>"Though the portal is unstable. The longer we wait, the more likely it spawns more. Your choice—I can answer questions now, or seal it first and explain after. Either way, I'm not going anywhere."

## Who She Is 

Mood:** Matter-of-fact, hint of weariness **Beat:** Celestial. Came to stop something. Failed. Doesn't oversell or dramatize.

> "I'm Aethera. A Celestial—one of the beings from beyond your sky. As for why I'm _here_..."

> "Something dangerous was sealed beneath this place. I came to stop it from escaping. I failed."

## When They Ask What Happened

**Mood:** Teaching, serious **Beat:** Keth'vyss backstory - Ascended → Darkin. Portal magic danger. Sets up future threat.

>"This was once a temple dedicated to Keth'vyss one of Shurima's Ascended, long ago. But like so many of the Ascended he was corrupted during the war of Icathia, transformed into Darkin."

[beat]

>"Before his corruption, Keth'vyss studied portal magic a dangerous practice these portals can be found and utilized by other creatures, as you have seen today. Every portal is a potential doorway for other entities to cross through, especially if left open or improperly sealed. Most mages who attempt portal magic either fail completely or create tears that collapse on themselves."

>"Now imagine that knowledge combined with Darkin power, and a mind that's been driven mad by millennia of imprisonment."


## What happened to her

**Mood:** Disturbed, genuinely unsettled **Beat:** Zilean didn't fight possession - welcomed it. This confuses her. Wasn't prepared.

>"A mage freed him. But instead of trying to control the power... he _let_ it consume him. Offered himself willingly. I've never seen anything like it."

>"I arrived as the possession was completing. I expected to face a desperate mage or a newly-freed Darkin. Instead, I found them merged—mind and power working as one. The mage wasn't being controlled. He was... choosing this."

>"I don't know why. And I wasn't prepared for what they could do together."


## Questions

**Mood:** Warm, curious, genuine interest **Beat:** Turns focus to them. Learns names carefully. Wants to understand their motivations.

>"But that's enough about this place's troubles. You came here to help—you stayed to fight. I'd like to know who I owe my thanks to."

[When they give their names, she repeats each one carefully, as if committing them to memory]

>**"[Name], [Name], [Name], [Name], [Name] Thank you."**

>"What brought you here? To Stonewall, to this temple? Most would have turned back when they saw the corruption."

>"And what is it you're trying to accomplish? Beyond investigating this place. Everyone has something driving them—a goal, a purpose."


## The Partnership Offer

**Mood:** Honest, vulnerable, hopeful **Beat:** Asks for help directly. Admits weakness. Offers little now, promises more later. Sees something in them

> "I need your help. And I know that's presumptuous—we've only just met, and you've already saved me once."

_[beat]_

> "I'm trapped here until I recover enough strength to leave this barrier. And even then, I'll be... diminished. To regain what I've lost, I need items of power. Magical artifacts, essences like that creature—anything with significant arcane energy."

> "Right now, I can offer you very little. Knowledge. Answers to questions, if I have them. That's all."

> "But as I recover... I can do more. Blessings. Protection. Magic that most mortals will never see. The stronger I become, the more I can give you in return."

> "And beyond what I can _give_ you... I would very much like to see what you become. You chose to enter this place. You chose to fight. Most would have fled. That kind of courage—"

> "—is rarer than you know."


### The Starwoven Bands

**Mood:** Gift-giving, slight vulnerability **Beat:** Tracking bracelets framed as protection. Asks permission - respects boundaries.

> "Before you go—or before you rest, if you're staying—I have something for you."

_[She produces 5 slender bands of silver, delicate but solid]_

> "These won't protect you from everything. But they'll help. And they'll let me... know you're safe. From a distance."
