
Every surface writhes with corruption—walls pulse, ceiling breathes, floor squelches underfoot. The smell is overwhelming: the same burnt copper, wet stone, cloying sweetness, and the thick stench of death and decay coating your throat with every breath.

At the back of the chamber: a cocoon of crystalline light pressed against the curved wall. The corruption strains toward it but cannot breach. Within, barely visible, is a feminine figure suspended as if sleeping.

In the center bodies lay Fifty, sixty, maybe more. Behind a portal tears at reality—a wound bleeding purple light. Growing from it is a twisted eggshell of pale bone-like matter has cracked open, fragments scattered across the floor. Whatever was inside has hatched.

Behind the pile you hear a wet crunching sound.


**After they walk in**

A creature jumps on the pile of bodies. Humanoid with sleek chitinous plates cover limbs that bend at too many angles. Whip-like appendages extend from its back. Its face is covered in blood still dripping and framed by tendrils that drift upward like hair underwater.

Two or three Voidlings skitter across the walls and ceiling, pausing to watch.
