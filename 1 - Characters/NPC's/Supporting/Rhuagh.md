## Background

Rhuagh is an ancient golden dragon who has lived in these tunnels for longer than Noxus has existed. In ages past, when the Valheru ruled and dragons served as their mounts across the cosmos, Rhuagh was bonded to a lesser Dragon Lord—not Ashen-Shugar, but one of the many who fell during the Chaos War. When the Valheru were banished to the Void, he felt the bond sever like a wound that never fully healed. The other dragons fled south or scattered across the world, but Rhuagh remained.

He settled in what had once been a Valheru lord's domain—ancient halls carved into the stone long before humans walked these lands. When the first tribes came, then the warlords, then the empire that built the Bastion above, Rhuagh remained in the depths. He used his magic to cloud the minds of treasure seekers and turn away those who wandered too deep. Those whose minds proved too strong to cloud, he killed—until he sickened of the killing and vowed to take no more lives.

Over the centuries, he accumulated a hoard from those he turned away and from expeditions into the deeper ruins. Among his treasures: a sword and armor he found in a collapsed Valheru hall, items that resonated with something he couldn't name. He kept them, though he never knew why. They felt like they were waiting for someone.

Now, after millennia of solitude in the dark, his time has finally come. He is too weak to cloud minds, too old to threaten. He simply waits for death in the halls that have been his home since before human memory.

## Appearance & Mannerisms

- **Age:** Ancient (several millennia old)
- **Race:** Golden Dragon
- **Appearance:** Magnificent but clearly aged, scales dulled with time, eyes still bright with wisdom
- **Speech Pattern:** Uses archaic "thee," "thou," "thy" consistently, speaks with formal dragon dignity
- **Death Watch Request:** Asks the party to sit his death watch since no other dragons are near to call
- **Notable Features:** Despite his weakness, maintains the dignified bearing of ancient nobility

## Personality Traits

- **Accepting of Death:** Views his approaching end as natural and proper, not something to fear or fight
- **Generous in Legacy:** Wants his treasures to serve a purpose rather than gathering dust in forgotten halls
- **Wise but Weary:** Carries the knowledge of ages but is tired from the weight of so much history and solitude
- **Keeper of Old Places:** Has guarded these halls for millennia—not out of greed, but because someone had to remember
- **Reformed Killer:** Once slew those who intruded; now sickened by violence and glad he lacks the strength for it
- **Discerning Judge:** Can sense the true nature of hearts—will only personally address those with genuine goodness
- **Ancient Dignity:** Maintains formal, archaic speech befitting his great age and wisdom
- **Fragmented Memory:** His memories of the Valheru era are incomplete—he remembers emotions and impressions more than specifics

## Campaign Story

### Centuries of Solitude (Background)

Has lived in the tunnels beneath the Bastion since before Noxus existed. Accumulated his hoard over millennia, including the sword and armor he found in deeper Valheru ruins. Turned away or killed intruders until he grew weary of killing and simply hid instead.

### Meeting the Party (Current)

The party, fleeing through the tunnels after Erlind's death, stumbles upon the dying dragon in his ancient hall. For the first time in centuries, visitors have found him—and he is too weak to turn them away, too tired to care. He asks them to sit his death watch, knowing he has only hours remaining.

### The Death Watch (Current)

Final conversation about life, death, and legacy during his last hours. He speaks of the old world in fragments—impressions of masters who rode dragons across the stars, a war that shook reality, the long lonely centuries since.

### The Final Gifts (Current)

Before passing, bequeaths specific treasures to worthy heroes. The sword and armor go to Marduun—Rhuagh doesn't know why these items feel meant for him, only that they do. "Thy heart is good, and some things become clear only when the time is right."

### Legacy (Future)

The deeper tunnels remain unexplored—Rhuagh hints that there are older places still, halls he never dared enter, where things older than dragons sleep. A potential future quest location if the party returns.

## The Three Treasures

**Chest of Gold:** A substantial fortune in various currencies—treasures accumulated over millennia that no longer matter to a dying dragon.

**The Ancient Weapon:** Contains [[Ashen-Shugar's Sword and Armor]]—Rhuagh doesn't know the sword's full history. He acquired it long ago from Valheru ruins, kept it because it resonated with something he couldn't name. It's specifically meant for Marduun, though neither dragon nor man understands why.

**Chest of Wonders:** Magical items, rare gems, and artifacts collected over centuries—gifts for the other party members who sit his death watch.

## What Rhuagh Knows (and Doesn't Know)

**He Remembers:**

- The Valheru existed and ruled the world in ages past
- Dragons served them, bonded to them, flew with them across the cosmos
- A great war ended their dominion—he felt the bonds sever across the world
- The tunnels beneath the Bastion are older than human memory

**He Doesn't Know:**

- The specific identity of Ashen-Shugar or his role in the Chaos War
- Why the sword resonates with Marduun specifically
- The nature of the temporal paradox connecting past and future

## Conversation Suggestions

### On His Death Watch

_"I have but hours remaining, and it is the way of my kind to sit the deathwatch, but there are none of my kind near enough to call. I would ask thee to wait for my passing before thy leaving. Wilt thou?"_

### On Death and Acceptance

_"I have had all that one of my kind could hope for: health, mates, young, riches, and rest. These are all I have ever wanted, and I have had them. The death hour matters not—it is still the same."_

### On the Old World (Fragmented)

_"There were masters once, in the time before time had meaning. They rode upon our backs across the black between stars. I remember... I remember the feeling of it. The cold and the fire. The hunger that was not hunger. But the faces... the names... these have faded."_

### On the Sword

_"I found it long ago, in ruins older than this fortress above us. It called to me, though I know not why. I have kept it since, waiting—though I knew not what I waited for. Perhaps thee."_

### On Why He Stayed

_"When the masters fell and the bonds were severed, the other dragons fled. South, east, to the far corners of the world. But I... I could not leave. This place was his, my master's. And when he was gone, it became mine. Someone had to remember. Someone had to keep the old places."_

### The Final Bequest (to good-hearted party members)

_"Before I pass, I have gifts for thee. In this chest is a gift for thee. Thou mayest take whatever else here pleaseth thee, for I will have no use for any of it. But of all in this hall, that is what I wish thee to have."_

### To Marduun Specifically

_"Marduun, thanks to thee for spending my last with me. In the box here is something for thee. I cannot say why it must be thine—only that it must. Thy heart is good, and some things become clear only when the time is right."_
