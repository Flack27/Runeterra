## Background

**The Tower Outside Time:** Zilean survived Icathia's fall ~3000 years ago by removing his tower and everyone inside from temporal existence. Everyone within froze in stasis except him—able to drift through past, present, and future but forever anchored to that frozen moment. For millennia he walked among his frozen people, searching countless timelines for a way to undo Icathia's destruction. Every path ended the same: the Void consumed everything.

**The Vision of Doom:** ~800 years ago, his temporal sight revealed something worse—Aethera, a corrupted Celestial, sought an ancient weapon of catastrophic power. The visions were fragmented, contradictory, but clear enough: the weapon was connected to the Void, perhaps even created by it. If she succeeded, all life on Runeterra would end. Even his tower's temporal displacement wouldn't protect against total annihilation. His frozen Icathians, preserved for three thousand years, would cease to exist.

**The Fragmentary Search:** For 800 years Zilean searched desperately through time for any intervention. The visions remained maddeningly incomplete—he could see Aethera searching, sense the weapon's apocalyptic purpose, but names, origins, the full scope remained obscured by temporal paradoxes his own future actions would create. In some timelines, he glimpsed a mighty warrior figure—ancient, powerful, perhaps the key to everything—but couldn't see who, or how, or why. Just that in those branches, there was a chance.

**The Only Solution:** Thousands of possibilities explored, all failures. Until he found one terrible thread: at Stonewall, if he let a sealed Darkin possess him, their fusion might wound Aethera enough to buy time. Not a good solution—he'd lose his body, unleash a mad scholar upon the world—but the only timeline where Aethera was delayed long enough for that mysterious warrior to emerge.

**The Sacrifice:** Zilean left his tower's safety for the first time in three thousand years, traveled to Stonewall, and broke Keth'vyss's seal himself. The fusion of chronomancer and Darkin created something powerful enough to catastrophically wound the Celestial, forcing her to seal herself. But Keth'vyss claimed total control and fled to Ionia. Zilean became a prisoner in his own body—aware but powerless, watching the Darkin open portals in the name of "research," knowing he'd abandoned his people forever but praying his incomplete gamble would somehow be enough.

**What He Endured:**
- Three thousand years walking among frozen loved ones, knowing their names and stories
- Eight hundred years searching through fragmentary visions, watching every clear path fail
- Years trapped in his own body, fighting constantly against Darkin control
- Never knowing if his sacrifice would be enough, or if he doomed the world by unleashing Keth'vyss

## Appearance & Mannerisms

- **Age:** ~3000+ years old (Icathia survivor)
- **Race:** Human Chronomancer
- **Current Appearance:** His body is used by Keth'vyss - aged but with Darkin corruption showing through (glowing eyes, dark energy threading through him)
- **Speech Pattern (After Liberation):** Measured, patient, carries the weight of millennia. Speaks to others with gentle authority, like a grandfather to children—not condescending, but with the calm certainty of someone who has seen empires rise and fall
- **Personality Traits:**
  - Everyone else feels like children to him (mortals live such brief spans)
  - Sometimes scolds without meaning to, then apologizes for treating grown heroes like students
  - Carries immense responsibility with quiet dignity
  - Patient but direct—three thousand years taught him not to waste time on pleasantries
  - Warm beneath the weariness, genuinely cares despite his age
- **Notable Features:** Eyes that have seen too much, hands that tremble slightly from the years of imprisonment, but a presence that commands respect through sheer accumulated wisdom

## Campaign Story

### Possessed State (Act 2)

When the party encounters Keth'vyss in Ionia, they're actually fighting both entities—though only the Darkin's personality is in control. Zilean is aware, trapped, desperately trying to influence events but unable to break through.

Occasionally, in moments of extreme stress or when Keth'vyss is distracted, Zilean's consciousness might flicker to the surface:
- A moment of clarity in the Darkin's eyes
- A warning whispered in an older, wearier voice: "Run, children. Please."
- Temporal magic that protects rather than harms
- Hesitation before opening a particularly dangerous portal

These glimpses are brief and painful—Zilean fighting against the overwhelming presence of the Darkin, knowing he's too weak to maintain control.

### Liberation (Act 4)

When Aethera finally confronts and absorbs Keth'vyss to gain his knowledge and power, the Darkin's consciousness is pulled from Zilean's body entirely. Zilean collapses, finally free but utterly spent. The possession lasted years—his body aged, his power depleted, his mind fractured from the constant internal war.

**First Words Upon Waking:**
> "Is it... over? Did the warrior appear? Please tell me my gamble meant something."

He's disoriented, weak, but the first thing he does is try to understand if his sacrifice worked. When he learns the party is that warrior's group—that they carry the ancient power he glimpsed in his visions—relief and wonder cross his face.

### Discovery of the Bard's Work (Act 4)

After recovering enough to use his temporal sight again, Zilean senses something unusual about Bennar—a temporal anchor, a deliberate manipulation of causality that he didn't create. The Bard Chime resonates with time magic similar to his own but fundamentally different in approach.

**His Reaction:**
> "Extraordinary... I thought I worked alone all these centuries. But someone else—someone with greater foresight than even I possessed—was placing anchors. Carefully. Precisely. I made one desperate sacrifice. They wove an entire tapestry. I am... humbled."

He studies the Chime with academic fascination, realizing his "lucky" success was actually orchestrated by another chronomancer playing a longer game. He doesn't feel diminished by this—rather, grateful that someone wiser was watching.

### The Great Revelation (Act 4)

Using his temporal magic and freedom to act, Zilean studies the party—particularly Marduun, whose fusion creates temporal ripples that intrigue him. He senses crucial memories are locked away, suppressed by something beyond normal means.

**Approaching the Topic:**
> "Forgive an old man's directness, but you carry something ancient within you. Something that creates paradoxes in time itself. There are memories locked away—not forgotten, but sealed. If you'll permit me, I believe together we might unlock them. You deserve to know the full truth of what you've become."

Working together with the now-fused Marduun/Ashen-Shugar, Zilean uses his chronomantic power to unlock the suppressed memories. What's revealed changes everything: the complete story of the Chaos War, the Lifestone's true purpose (the "weapon" from his visions), why the Watchers (the Void entities he sensed) hate Ashen-Shugar specifically, and the full scope of the temporal paradox.

**After the Revelation:**
> "So that's what I saw. That mighty warrior was you—is you—was always you. The Valheru, the Watchers, the paradox that made you different from your kind... it all makes a terrible kind of sense now. Aethera seeks to use the Lifestone to open a path for the Watchers' return. That is the apocalypse I saw. And you, child, are the key to stopping it—because you already did, millennia ago."

This revelation shows the party the complete picture—why Aethera needs Keth'vyss's portal knowledge, what she plans at the Lifestone, and why stopping her is the only way to prevent the Watchers' return.

## GM Notes

### Roleplaying Zilean (Macros-Style)

**Key Characteristics:**
- Speaks with the authority of three thousand years, but not arrogantly
- Treats party like beloved students/grandchildren—sometimes scolds, then catches himself
- "Forgive me. When you've lived as long as I have, everyone seems like children. It's a poor excuse for my sharp tongue."
- Patient with genuine questions, impatient with foolishness
- Direct and honest—too old for games or manipulation
- Carries grief and responsibility with quiet dignity
- Warm and caring beneath the ancient weariness

**Example Dialogue:**

After party makes a reckless decision:
> "What were you *thinking*? No, don't answer—I can see you weren't. [sighs] Forgive me. At my age, everyone seems like wayward children in need of guidance. You're heroes in your own right. I simply... worry. I've lost too much already."

When asked why he didn't explain something earlier:
> "Because you needed to learn it yourselves. I could tell you the answer to every question, child, but that wouldn't help you when I'm gone. And make no mistake—I won't last forever. The possession took its toll. Teach a man to fish, as they say."

### The Weight of Incomplete Knowledge

Even after liberation, Zilean doesn't know everything. He saw:
- A corrupted Celestial seeking a world-ending weapon
- Fragments showing it's connected to the Void
- Glimpses of a mighty ancient warrior being the key
- Timelines where everything succeeds or fails

He DIDN'T see:
- That the weapon was called the Lifestone
- That it was created by the Valheru
- That the Valheru became the Watchers
- How the temporal paradox actually works
- The Bard's full plan

This keeps him humble and allows the party/Marduun to teach HIM things during the revelation, creating collaborative discovery rather than exposition dump.

### Why His Visions Were Fragmentary

1. **His Own Actions Created Paradoxes:** The moment he decided to intervene created timeline branches that made prediction impossible
2. **The Temporal Loop:** Ashen-Shugar's influence from the future affected the past, creating circular causality Zilean couldn't fully parse
3. **The Bard's Interference:** Another chronomancer's work created "blind spots" in Zilean's sight
4. **Aethera's Celestial Nature:** Her corruption created temporal anomalies that distorted prediction
5. **The Watchers' Alien Nature:** Void entities exist outside normal causality, making them hard to perceive through time magic

### Combat Abilities

**While Possessed (Act 2):**
- Darkin strength and hemomancy
- Portal magic and dimensional manipulation  
- Zilean's temporal abilities (twisted by Darkin control)
- Can fight across multiple timelines simultaneously
- Regeneration through blood magic

**After Liberation (Act 4):**
- Weakened but still powerful time magic
- Support-focused rather than combat
- Can slow/speed time for allies and enemies
- Temporal resurrection (party members who die can be brought back if he acts quickly)
- Can study and unlock temporal mysteries
- Crucial for understanding the paradox and unlocking memories

### The Tower's Fate

The tower remains frozen outside time, waiting. Zilean knows he abandoned his people—faces he knew for three thousand years, frozen mid-scream as the Void consumed them. If the campaign succeeds and Runeterra is saved, perhaps he can finally return and find a way to free them. If it fails, the tower will cease to exist along with everything else—the final, bitter confirmation that his sacrifice wasn't enough.

**When Asked About His People:**
> "They're still there. Waiting. I see their faces every time I close my eyes. I promised them I would find a way... and then I left them. For you. For this. It had to be enough. It *must* be enough."
