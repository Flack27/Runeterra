
_Swain's eyes drift to Bennar. His head tilts slightly, as if listening to something only he can hear. A faint smile crosses his face—not warm, not cruel, simply... knowing._



_Swain studies them for a long moment. When he speaks, his voice is measured—not loud, but carrying the weight of absolute certainty._

"Let me teach you something the old nobility never understood. They believed trust must be earned through years of service, through blood ties, through elaborate games of loyalty. They were fools. Trust earned that way can be revoked the same way—it depends on the other person's actions. It makes you reactive. Weak.

I have decided you are capable. That decision is mine. It does not depend on whether you prove worthy of it. It does not depend on whether you succeed or fail. It does not depend on whether you one day betray me.

You may fail. You may lie. You may scheme against me the moment you leave this room—that would be your nature, and I expect nothing else from anyone. But my judgment? That remains mine. I do not unmake my decisions because the world fails to cooperate with them.

Stonewall is yours. Not because you've earned my trust. Because I've chosen to place it."

_He turns away, the audience clearly over._

"Prove me right, and Noxus gains a valuable asset. Prove me wrong, and I will correct my error with the same certainty I made it."
