## Background

**Who She Was:** Aethera was a Celestial whose sacred duty was to nurture and guide mortal civilizations. As the Tending Light, she watched over emerging societies across Runeterra, helping them through their formative stages—not through direct intervention, but through subtle guidance and cosmic influence. She witnessed the birth of empires, the flowering of cultures, and the endless potential of mortal life. She loved all of humanity equally, without bias or favoritism, seeing each civilization as a precious bloom in the cosmic garden.

**The Fall of Icathia:** When Icathia rebelled against Shurima, Aethera watched with growing dread. As tensions escalated and the Icathians made their desperate choice to summon the Void, she descended to stop the catastrophe. She witnessed the people she had nurtured for centuries willingly open a door that should never be opened, summoning oblivion itself. The Ascended warriors who came to stop the breach were driven mad by what they fought. The land was consumed, twisted, corrupted beyond recognition.

Aethera dove into the rift itself to contain it—saving the rest of Runeterra but condemning Icathia to isolation. In doing so, she was directly exposed to the raw essence of the Void. Unlike mortals who break quickly, her Celestial nature resisted the corruption, but could not fully reject it. She succeeded in her mission, but was forever changed.

**The Slow Corruption (~3000 years to ~800 years ago):** The Void infection worked slowly through her essence like spiritual poison. She continued her duties, but something fundamental had broken. She couldn't forget Icathia—couldn't stop asking _why_ they had done it. Over the following millennia, she witnessed the pattern repeat:

- The Darkin War (Ascended driven mad from the same Void exposure she'd witnessed)
- Mordekaiser's realm of torment
- The Ruination (a king breaking reality itself for selfish love)
- Countless smaller tragedies of mortals reaching for power they couldn't control

Each event reinforced the terrible pattern: mortals, when desperate or ambitious, inevitably open doors that should remain closed. The Void corruption twisted her perception, warping her loving nature into something tragic. Her nurturing instinct remained, but the logic became corrupted.

**The Last 800 Years:** By the rise of Noxus, her corruption had deepened to the point where she began searching for a solution. The Lifestone became her answer—a weapon that could end the cycle swiftly and mercifully.

**Her Conclusion:** After centuries of processing trauma and watching the pattern repeat, Aethera reached a devastating conclusion: mortals will _always_ eventually summon catastrophe. It is in their nature to reach beyond their grasp, to open forbidden doors when desperate. She cannot save them from themselves. The kindest thing—the most merciful thing—is to end the cycle before another Icathia, before another Ruination, before the next horror that they will have to suffer.

The Lifestone became her solution: instant, absolute, painless. No slow corruption, no drawn-out suffering, no time to feel fear. In her corrupted logic, it is the most merciful genocide tool that exists. Better one swift end than endless cycles of horror.

**Current State:** Aethera is approximately 70% corrupted—her personality and love for mortals remain genuine, but her reasoning has been poisoned. She genuinely cares for every human, including the party. She is immensely saddened by what she believes must be done, but sees it as necessary mercy.

## Campaign Story

### Pre-Campaign: The Confrontation

Aethera came to Stonewall after sensing [[Keth'vyss]]'s presence—a Darkin scholar whose research into [[Valheru]] portal magic made him perfect for her needs. [[Keth'vyss]] had been sealed in the ancient shrine beneath [[Stonewall]], and she intended to absorb him to gain his portal expertise

[[Zilean]], having glimpsed fragments of her plan through his time visions, arrived and allowed Keth'vyss to take control of his body. The Zilean/Keth'vyss fusion—combining a chronomancer's power with a Darkin's ancient strength—engaged her in battle. While not enough to kill a Celestial, their combined assault caught her off-guard. Zilean's temporal magic disrupted her essence while Keth'vyss struck with Darkin ferocity.

### First Encounter (Session 1)

When the party investigates the shrine after Stonewall's abandonment, they encounter Aethera in her sealed state. She is honest about her identity as a Celestial, though she doesn't reveal her true purpose. She explains that she came to stop a dangerous Darkin named Keth'vyss who threatens the region with his mad experiments opening Void portals. She tells them that Keth'vyss attacked her and fled, leaving her severely wounded. She asks for their help in recovering her strength so she can pursue him and stop his reckless portal openings before they doom the region.

### Deception Phase (Act 1-2)

Aethera genuinely helps and cares for the party during this time. Her kindness is not an act—she truly loves them as she loves all mortals. She grants boons, offers guidance, and protects them when she can. She needs their help gathering power (through offerings of monster parts, magical items, artifacts) to recover from her wounds.

As she grows stronger, the corruption deepens: 30% → 50% → 70%. Her lucid moments become rarer, but when they occur, she experiences deep sorrow at what she must do.

**What she tells the party:** She needs strength to defend against threats, to keep Stonewall safe, to help them on their heroic journey.

**What she doesn't tell them:** Every offering makes her strong enough to execute her plan. She genuinely wants them to succeed and grow stronger—she wants their final days to be fulfilling and meaningful.

### Act 2: Sending Them to Ionia

Aethera sends the party to Ionia, claiming Keth'vyss is a dangerous threat opening Void portals. This is partially true—Keth'vyss IS dangerous and IS opening portals. But her real goal is to eventually absorb him for his portal knowledge and Valheru essence fragments. She needs both to open a rift large enough for the Watchers to manifest and activate the Lifestone.

### Act 3: The Hunt and Revelation

When Aethera finally absorbs Keth'vyss, the fusion completes her corruption: 70% → 95%. Her last lucid moments are horror at what she's become, then full commitment to the plan. She still uses language of care and protection, but it rings hollow now.

The party discovers her true nature during the chase to the Lifestone. The revelation is devastating because her care for them was always genuine—she simply has a horrifying definition of "mercy."

### Final Confrontation

At the Lifestone, Aethera attempts to open the portal for the Watchers to activate the weapon. She fights not with hatred, but with sad determination. She sees the party as beloved children who don't understand what she's trying to save them from.
