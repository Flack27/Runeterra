Mukai grew up in a mountain village in the north-east of Ionia. His life was simple and quaint, and he hated every second of it. He grew bored of working in the farms with his father, and longed for excitement. When an old martial artist named Master Shun traveled through his village looking for pupils, Mukai discovered something worth living or; the thrill of battle. Without hesitation, he left his old village behind as Master Shun took him in.

They traveled to Shun's monastery, where Mukai started his training alongside several others. To Mukai's dismay, he didn't seem to perform above his peers. He wasn't performing badly, but there were several students who seemed much more talented than he did.
Frustrated, he turned to Shun's library, looking for insight and possible ways to overcome his peers.
He learned much, but again it was not enough to put him above his fellow students. 
One late night, Mukai's studies were interrupted by a hooded visitor in the library. Mukai had never seen him before, and the stranger refused to share his name. All he told Mukai was that there was knowledge and power that Shun was keeping from him. He then handed him a key, which was said to open a locked cabinet in the library holding all that Mukai was looking for.

The stranger left, and Mukai found the locket cabinet. Inside was a dusty old tome named 'The forbidden art of the Dark Hado'.
Greedily, he started reading and practicing the contents. Within less than two hours, Master Shun rushed into the library, clearly distressed. He scolded Mukai, explaining the dangers of the Dark Hado. It corrupted the soul and mind, and was a demonic way of fighting, never to be allowed. He forbade Mukai from ever using it again. Shun took the tome with him, and punished Mukai by making him meditate for a week, in a location slightly outside of the monastery grounds.
Mukai had however already learned some of the fundamentals of using the Dark Hado, and was letting it corrupt his life energy. He spent the week trying to harness it, discarding his master's warnings. It festered inside of him, and over the week he felt anger and resentment towards his master. How dare he keep this power away from him?
When he finished his punishment, he felt a different man. He was going to continue practicing the Dark Hado, and defeat Master Shun when he had fully mastered it. He was to show him that Mukai's resolve was stronger, and that Shun's fear of real power was a weakness.
Mukai's return was spoiled. Mourning students told Mukai that Master Shun had been defeated in battle, and murdered.
Mukai's anger boiled. His chance to prove himself was taken away. The tome of the Dark Hado had also been stolen by this same warrior.

Mukai asked for details of the warrior, packed his things, and set off to find this mysterious fighter. Being the one that bested his old master, this one was now his new target. He was going to prove himself one way or another.
